3D Print Log Pro

3D Print Log Pro is a paid subscription tier that removes ads and unlocks premium features across the entire application.

Pro Features

  • Ad-free experience — No advertisements anywhere in the application.
  • More images per print — Upload up to 20 images per print (free tier: 5 images).
  • File attachments — Attach files to your prints, including G-code, STL, and 3MF files.

Pricing

3D Print Log Pro is available on two billing plans:

  • Monthly — $2.99 per month
  • Annual — $29.99 per year (approximately $2.50 per month, saving about 16% compared to monthly)

How to Subscribe

Visit the Pro page and choose either the monthly or annual plan. You will be taken to a secure Stripe checkout page to complete your payment. Once the payment is confirmed, your account is upgraded to Pro immediately.

Managing Your Subscription

To cancel, update your payment method, or view past invoices, navigate to Settings, scroll to the Subscription section, and click Manage Billing. This opens the Stripe billing portal where you can make any changes to your subscription.
