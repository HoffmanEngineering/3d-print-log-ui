**Remaining material** is here! Open a saved material to see what is left on the spool and the recent prints that used it. Take a look at [your materials](/filament).

**More bulk actions** have landed on the print list. Select some prints to add them to a project, or change their visibility, printer or permissions in one go. On a phone, long-press a card to start selecting. Try it on [your prints](/prints).

Both features were asked for by users. Have an idea of your own? [Send in feedback](/feedback) and help shape what comes next.
