Filaments

3D Print Log Filaments helps you keep track of what materials and colors you have, and how much filament is left on the roll. Print with confidence knowing you won't run out of filament. Navigate to the Filament section to manage your rolls of Filament.

Filaments List

The Filament List shows you all your rolls of filament. Use the Search input at the top to filter the list by the filament's Name, Brand or Color fields. Use the Include Inactive checkbox to include Empty or Inactive rolls of filament. The table is sorted by Filament Remaining by default, but you can click on any of the table headers to order by Name, Brand, or Color.

The Filament List Table

  • Color Box - Displays the selected color of the filament.
  • Name - The Display Name of the Filament
  • Brand - The Brand of filament.
  • Color - The name of the Color of the filament.
  • Remaining (g) - The weight of Filament remaining on the roll.

How Remaining Filament is Calculated

The Remaining Filament is calculated by the Filament's [Initial Nominal Weight]-[sum of Filament used in Prints]+[sum of any Adjustments].

Initial Nominal Weight is entered when Adding/Editing a filament, and is the nominal, or expected, weight of the filament on the roll. So a 1kg roll of filament would have an Initial Nominal Weight of 1000 grams.

Filament Used in Prints are the weights entered in the Filament Section when Adding/Editing a print. See Prints Documentation for more details on how to add filament usage. When calculating Remaining Filament, a print's Actual Filament Used will be used if available, otherwise it'll use the Estimated Filament Used.

Filament Adjustments are entered when Adding/Editing a filament, and represent non-print usage of filament. Useful for documenting the wasted filament when changing colors, or adjusting a filament's weight when adding a partially used roll to 3D Print Log. See the Adjustments section below for more information.

Add a roll of Filament

From the Filament list, click on the button.

General Section

Give the roll of filament a Name used to identify the roll. If you have multiple rolls of the same brand/color, it may be helpful to give the roll a name with a number (example: Hatchbox Black 1).

The Material Type is for storing the type of filament. You can select one of the pre-populated material types to automatically fill the Material Density, or you can add any other material just by typing into the field. The Material Density is entered in Grams Per Cubic Centimeter, and used when calculating weights.

The Color section has a Color Picker that lets you select the color which is displayed. The Color Name lets you enter the manufacturer's name. The Brand field is for recording the filament's manufacturer. The Diameter is for the diameter of the filament in millimeters. The Notes section lets you record any additional notes/description/etc for the filament.

Weights Section

The Weights section lets you record the initial weight of the rolls, as well as any Weight Adjustments needed.

  • Initial Total Weight (g) - The total weight of the roll in grams, including the weight of the filament and the spool. Find by putting the entire spool on a scale.
  • Initial Nominal Weight (g) - The nominal, or expected, weight of just the filament in grams. For instance, a 1kg roll of filament would have a nominal weight of 1,000 grams.
  • Spool Weight (g) - The weight of the empty spool.

Filament Adjustments

Adjustments represent non-print usage of filament. Adjustment weights are added when calculating the remaining filament on a roll, so negative amounts represent the removal of filament from the spool. Examples of adjustments would be:

  • Estimating filament used when adding a partially-used roll to 3D Print Log.
  • Tracking filament lost when switching colors on a printer.

Temperatures

Record the recommended temperature and range in Celsius for this filament.

Purchase Details

Record the Purchase Date, Location, and Price for future reference, and ease of reordering. The Location can be used to store a URL to the online retailer, or the name of the store purchased from.

Edit a Filament

Click on any row in the Filament List to view and edit an existing 3D print.

After making changes, click to update that filament.

Delete a Filament

Filaments that have not been used in any Prints can be deleted from the Filament List. Click the ... more menu on the row for the filament you want to delete. A warning that the filament will be permanently deleted will appear. When Delete is clicked, the filament will be deleted.

If a filament has been used in a Print, you cannot delete it. Instead, you can Inactive a filament by Editing, then unchecking the Is Active checkbox in the General section. Inactive filament will not be displayed.

Loaded Filament

Printers can have one or more filament rolls considered "loaded", meaning they are currently in use by that printer. A roll can only be "loaded" by one printer at a time, so loading a roll into a new printer will automatically unload it from the old printer. See the Printer Documentation for more information on managing loaded filament.
