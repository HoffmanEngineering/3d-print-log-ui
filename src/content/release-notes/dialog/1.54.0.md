**Tap your phone to a spool** in the [Android app](/docs/android-app) to add its tag to a material, pick it on a print or open it with **Scan spool**. Bambu Lab, Snapmaker, Anycubic ACE and OpenSpool tags even fill in a new material for you. See [RFID / NFC Tags](/docs/materials#tags-android).

**Home Assistant** can show your spools, their remaining weight and a low-filament sensor through the Spoolman integration. See [Home Assistant](/docs/home-assistant-spoolman).
