Delete Account

While we are sad to see you go, you can deactivate your 3D Print Log account, and permanently delete all your data.

If you are deleting your account because of a feature that 3D Print Log is missing, please send us a feedback and let us know what you'd like to see. We are adding features all the time, and love to know how we can improve.

  • Deactivated accounts will have all data deleted 24 hours after deactivation. This includes any recorded Prints, Printers, Filaments, Comments, Images and your Profile.
  • Deletion is irreversible. Once deletion occurs, there is no way to recover your data if you change your mind.
  • You may create a new account at anytime in the future, but there will be no way to recover any deleted data.
