For one printer on a desk

Never lose a good setting again

You dialed in the temperature and speed six months ago. Log it once and it is still there — next to the photo, the duration, the filament you used and the note about what went wrong the first time.

For a bench full of them

Know what every spool has left

Track each roll and bottle from first use to empty. See what is running low before you start a twelve-hour print, and stop guessing whether there is enough left on the spool to finish.
