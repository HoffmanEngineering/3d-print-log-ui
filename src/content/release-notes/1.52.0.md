title: 'Achievements & Printer Photos'
---

**Achievements** have arrived. Logging prints, keeping a streak going, trying new materials and connecting your slicer now earn badges, each a little shield with tiers running from Bronze PLA up to Glow-in-the-Dark. Big moments get a celebration card and smaller ones a quick toast, and the new [Achievements](/achievements) page shows your whole collection with progress toward the next tier and how rare each badge is. Some badges stay hidden until you earn them. Your prints list suggests the next badge within reach, and you can feature your badges on your public profile. If celebrations aren't your thing, set them to Quiet or Off in [Settings](/settings). See the [Achievements documentation](/docs/achievements) for the full details.

**Printer photos** make it easy to tell your machines apart at a glance. Add photos to any printer, star the one you want as its default, and that photo appears next to the printer's name everywhere it shows up: the print list and cards, print details, the printer picker, maintenance and analytics. Printer photos are private and never appear on a print's public page. See [Printer photos](/docs/printers#printer_photos) for how to add them.

Uploading G-code from a slicer we don't recognize by name also got better. Forks like Snapmaker Orca, Elegoo Slicer and SuperSlicer are now matched to the closest supported slicer, so their settings, print time and per-slot filament usage are read directly instead of falling back to estimating from the toolpath.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Achievements** - Earn badges for printing milestones, streaks, materials and more, with tiers from Bronze PLA to Glow-in-the-Dark. Streaks count days in your own time zone. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189), [API PR #115](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/115))
- **Achievement celebrations** - A celebration card for big moments and a toast for everything else, shown as soon as the badge is earned and only once even with several tabs open. Choose Full, Quiet (no confetti or motion) or Off in Settings. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Achievements page** - Your collection grouped by category, with progress bars, rarity and a shareable detail view for each badge. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Badges on public profiles** - Feature your badges on your public profile, and visitors can browse your full collection. Turn it off in Settings. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189), [API PR #115](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/115))
- **Next achievement hint** - The prints list suggests the next badge within reach; dismiss it if you'd rather not see it. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Achievement push notifications** - A new "Achievement earned" toggle in the mobile app's push notification settings. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Fixed notification links** - Notifications that link to a page with options (such as a specific badge) now open correctly from the bell and the notifications page. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Fixed notification polling** - Logging out and back in no longer stacks duplicate notification checks, and a single failed check no longer stops notifications for the rest of the session. ([PR #189](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/189))
- **Printer photos** - Add photos to your printers and pick a default. Files are checked for size and type before uploading, and a failed upload can be retried without creating a duplicate printer. ([PR #176](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/176), [API PR #114](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/114))
- **Printer photos everywhere** - A printer's default photo appears beside its name on the print list, print cards, grouped view, print details, the printer picker, maintenance and analytics. ([PR #176](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/176))
- **Smarter slicer detection** - G-code from an unrecognized slicer is matched to the closest supported one (Snapmaker Orca, Elegoo, SuperSlicer and other forks), with a note telling you which it used. ([PR #183](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/183))
- **Per-slot filament usage** - OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer MMU/XL uploads now log usage for each filament slot. Bambu Studio files are read directly without the toolpath estimate dialog. ([PR #183](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/183))
- **Cura and Anycubic parsing fixes** - Cura files without a settings block no longer fail to upload, and Nylon now uses the correct density for weight estimates. ([PR #183](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/183))
- **Redesigned G-code analysis dialog** - When a file has to be estimated from its toolpath, a clearer dialog shows the steps, a single progress bar and per-extruder filament rows. ([PR #183](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/183))
- **New documentation** - A new [Achievements](/docs/achievements) page, a [Printer photos](/docs/printers#printer_photos) section, and [Create a print from a G-code file](/docs/prints#from-gcode) on the Prints page.
