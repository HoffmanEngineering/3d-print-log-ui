**Push notifications** are here! If you send print events to 3D Print Log from OctoPrint or Klipper, your phone can now tell you the moment a print finishes or fails. Pick what you want to hear about in [Settings](/settings). The updated Android app that receives them is rolling out to users soon.

What else should we notify you about? A spool running low, a printer due for maintenance, something we have not thought of? [Send in your feedback](/feedback) and help shape what comes next.

Printing QR labels is also much easier: the sheet now lays itself out to fit your paper and label size, and remembers your choices for next time.
