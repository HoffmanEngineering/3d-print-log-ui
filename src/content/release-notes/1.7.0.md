title: '1.7- Filament Roll Tracking 🎉'
---

Filament tracking is here! Now you can add rolls of filament, and 3D Print Log will automatically keep track of how much filament is remaining on the roll. See colors, print temperatures, and record brand and purchasing information for all your rolls of filament.

- Added new [Filament](/filament) page, where you can manage your rolls of filament
- Updated the [Print Details](/prints) page to have a **Filament Usage** section for assigning a roll of filament to a print.

See the [Filament Documentation](/docs/filaments) and [Prints Documentation](/docs/prints) pages for more info. Happy Printing!
