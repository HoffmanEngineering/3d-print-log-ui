#### CSV Exporting

This is your data, you should be able to use it how you like! Whether you want to backup your data to your computer, or import it into a spreadsheet for your own analytics, 3D Print Log has you covered. You can now export all your prints as a .csv (comma separated values) file. Visit your <a [routerLink]="['/settings']">Settings</a> and click **Export** to save your print report.
