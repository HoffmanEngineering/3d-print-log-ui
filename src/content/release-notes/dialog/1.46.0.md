**The new print page** is here! Your photos are front and center now, with all the details right next to them: printer, status, times and costs at a glance, every filament you used (shown in its actual color), and a link to the project it belongs to.

**Bulk editing** has landed on the print list too. Tick off the prints you want, then mark them all done or delete them in one go. Finished a whole plate overnight? That used to be about eighteen clicks, now it is two. Go check out [your prints](/prints), or read [the Prints documentation](/docs/prints).
