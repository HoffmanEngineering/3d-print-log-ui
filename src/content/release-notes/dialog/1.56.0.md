**Bridges are here!** Manage your printlog-bridge from the new Bridges screen: see the printers it found on your network, connect them in a couple of clicks, and enter their passwords encrypted to your bridge. Bambu Lab, PrusaLink, Elegoo, OctoPrint, Duet, FlashForge and Anycubic printers are all supported. See [Bridges](/docs/printlog-bridge-bridges).

**Resin prints now show mL** on print and printer pages, and your analytics and project totals show mL beside the grams.
