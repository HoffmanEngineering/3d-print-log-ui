title: 'Bridges Show Your Printer Names'
---

On the [Bridges](/docs/printlog-bridge-bridges) page, printers your bridge watches from its config file now show the 3D Print Log printer they log to, with a link to its page, instead of the name the printer advertises on your network (or only its address). This needs printlog-bridge 0.2.1 or later.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Printer names on the Bridges page** - Printers in a bridge's config file show and link their 3D Print Log printer, with the config file's name beside the address. ([PR #425](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/425), [API PR #307](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/307))
