**A guided start for new accounts.** New sign-ups now land on a short welcome that walks them through adding their printer, the spool that's loaded in it, and their first print (by hand, from a slicer, or from Klipper or OctoPrint). Every step can be skipped. Afterwards, a Getting Started checklist on the [Prints](/docs/prints) list shows what's left, and a Resend button helps anyone whose confirmation email went missing. Existing accounts see no change.

**Pick your printer from a catalog of 900+ models.** When you add a printer, choose one of the popular printers or search the catalog (built from Cura and OrcaSlicer), and its specs such as build volume, nozzle and filament diameter, and heated bed are filled in for you. You can still adjust any of them, or enter a printer by hand. When you upload G-code, the printer named in the file is matched against the catalog to suggest which of your printers ran it. See [Printers](/docs/printers).

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Welcome steps for new accounts** - New accounts are guided through adding a printer, the loaded spool and a first print, with per-slicer setup lines. Skip setup works from every step. ([PR #352](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/352), [API PR #269](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/269))
- **Getting Started checklist** - The Prints list shows what's left to set up until it's done or dismissed. ([PR #352](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/352), [API PR #269](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/269))
- **Resend the confirmation email** - If your email isn't confirmed yet, you can send the confirmation email again, and a confirmed address is picked up without signing out. ([PR #352](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/352), [API PR #269](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/269))
- **Add a printer from the print form** - The printer list on a print now has "+ Add a printer", which keeps everything a slicer already filled in. ([PR #352](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/352))
- **Printer catalog** - Search 900+ printers from Cura and OrcaSlicer, or pick a popular one, when adding a printer. Its specs are filled in from the catalog. ([PR #354](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/354), [API PR #270](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/270))
- **Find your printer** - The full printer form can link an existing printer to its catalog model and shows which specs come from it. ([PR #354](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/354), [API PR #270](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/270))
- **Printer detection from G-code** - An uploaded file's printer name is matched against the catalog to suggest which of your printers ran the print. Your own choice always wins. ([PR #354](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/354), [API PR #270](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/270))
- **Achievement celebrations wait for the page** - After saving a print, the celebration now opens over your Prints list instead of an empty page. ([PR #355](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/355))
- **My Profile link** - The menu's My Profile link no longer points nowhere before your profile loads. ([PR #352](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/352))
- **Home Assistant integration guide** - A new guide to the upcoming 3D Print Log Home Assistant integration, with clearer install steps. ([PR #348](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/348), [PR #349](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/349))
- **Spoolman warning threshold** - The [Home Assistant via Spoolman](/docs/home-assistant-spoolman) guide now explains how the warning threshold also changes when the integration's events fire. ([PR #347](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/347))
