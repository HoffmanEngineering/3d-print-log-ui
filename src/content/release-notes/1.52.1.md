Getting ready for email. 3D Print Log will soon send a few emails about your prints (onboarding tips, a monthly recap, and a heads-up when a printer goes quiet). None are going out yet, but you can choose which ones you want ahead of time in the new Email section of [Settings](/settings). Every email will also have a link to change your preferences or unsubscribe in one click.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Email settings** - A new Email section in [Settings](/settings) shows the address we send to and lets you turn each kind of email on or off, or all of them at once. ([PR #193](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/193))
- **One-time email notice** - A short banner lets you know about the upcoming emails, with a shortcut to your email settings. ([PR #193](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/193))
- **Manage emails without signing in** - Links in an email open a page where you can change your preferences or unsubscribe without signing in. ([PR #193](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/193))
- **Privacy** - Email addresses and email links are kept out of usage analytics, and the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy-policy) now covers email. ([PR #193](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/193))
- **Email artwork** - Logos and achievement badge images for the upcoming emails. ([PR #206](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/206))
- **New documentation** - A new [Email Notifications](/docs/email-notifications) page covers what we send, when, and how to turn it off. ([PR #193](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/193))
