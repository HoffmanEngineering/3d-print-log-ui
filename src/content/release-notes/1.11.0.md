title: 'Deleting User Accounts'
---

You are in control of your data, so now you can choose to delete your 3D Print Log account and all associated data. If you wish to delete your account, you can find the new options under **Delete Account** on your [Settings Page](/settings). After a 24 hour waiting period, your account and all prints, filament, printers, etc, will be permanently deleted from the website.
