Log and analyze your 3D Prints

Get started

3D Print Log is a simple tool for tracking 3D prints. Easily record details of your 3D printers, filament usage, print status, print duration and more.

View the quick start guide, or jump into it by creating a free account today and start logging!

Track Every 3D Print

Log details for every print. Upload pictures, track print times and filament usage, save notes and more. Search through past prints so you never forget a detail again.

Create Your Material Library

Keep track of every roll of filament and bottle of resin. See what colors and materials you have, record filament usage and know exactly how much material is remaining on each spool or in the bottle. Never start a print again wondering if there is enough material to finish.

View Print Analytics

See statistics about your prints. Metrics like total print times, material usage, and how often your prints succeed are just a click away.

Integrate with Cura and Octoprint

The 3D Print Log plugin for Ultimaker Cura makes it easy to send print information and settings directly from your slicer. And you can setup Octoprint to automatically send print status updates to 3D Print Log.
