title: 'NFC Spool Tags & Home Assistant'
---

**Tap your phone to a spool.** In the [Android app](/docs/android-app) (version 1.3.0 or later), hold the phone to a spool's RFID or NFC tag instead of typing its UID. Add a tag to a material, pick a material on a print just by tapping its spool, or tap **Scan spool** on the Materials list to open the spool in your hand. Bambu Lab, Snapmaker, Anycubic ACE and OpenSpool tags go further: the app reads the brand, material and color stored on the tag, suggests which of your materials it belongs to, or creates a new material already filled in from the tag and the SpoolmanDB filament database. See [RFID / NFC Tags](/docs/materials#tags-android).

**See your spools in Home Assistant.** Point the community Spoolman integration at 3D Print Log and Home Assistant shows each spool's remaining weight, color and material, plus a low-filament sensor. See [Home Assistant](/docs/home-assistant-spoolman).

Materials analytics now count **filament adjustments**, such as weigh-ins, as their own series, so corrections to a spool show up beside what your prints used. And when you come back after skipping a release or two, the version dialog now shows **every release you missed**, not just the newest.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Read spool tags in the Android app** - Add a tag to a material, pick a material by tapping its spool, and find a spool with **Scan spool** on the Materials list. ([PR #335](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/335))
- **See what a tag says** - Bambu Lab, Snapmaker, Anycubic ACE and OpenSpool tags show their brand, material and color, suggest which of your materials they belong to, and can create a new material filled in from the tag and SpoolmanDB. A material's page shows what each of its tags says. ([PR #343](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/343), [API PR #260](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/260))
- **Tag, You're It** - A new achievement for linking your first spool tag. ([PR #335](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/335), [API PR #244](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/244))
- **Home Assistant** - A new guide to showing your spools in Home Assistant through the Spoolman integration. ([PR #341](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/341))
- **Adjustments in Materials analytics** - Filament adjustments appear as their own series on the Materials tab, with net totals by type, brand and color, an Adjustments column in Top spools, and adjustment columns in the CSV exports. ([PR #342](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/342), [API PR #257](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/257))
- **Not tracked spools** - A spool with no starting weight now says "Not tracked" instead of "0g" in the materials list, printer details and the print editor. ([PR #338](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/338), [API PR #250](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/250))
- **Every missed release** - The version dialog rolls up every release since your last visit, and the menu marks release notes you haven't read yet. ([PR #336](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/336), [API PR #245](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/245))
- **Fixed saving a print with a scanned material** - A print whose material was picked by scanning its QR code or tag now saves, and a failed save shows an error. ([PR #344](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/344))
