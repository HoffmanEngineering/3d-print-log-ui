---

The main image on a private print's page shows again. Since 1.46.0 it read "Image unavailable" for the print's owner, even though the thumbnails below it loaded.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Private print images** - A private print's page loads its images with your sign-in, so you see them again, including when you pick another image from the thumbnails. ([PR #427](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/427))
