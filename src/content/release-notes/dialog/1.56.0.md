**Bridges are here!** The latest printlog-bridge now automatically detects printers on your network, and you can manage all your connected printers on the new [Bridges](/printers/bridges) page. Klipper, Bambu Lab, PrusaLink, Elegoo, OctoPrint, Duet, FlashForge and Anycubic printers are all supported.

**Resin prints now show mL** on print and printer pages, and your analytics and project totals show mL beside the grams.
