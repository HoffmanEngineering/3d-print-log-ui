# 3D Print Log

> Log and track your 3D prints, filament, and settings. Send prints straight from OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Creality Print, and Cura, and keep every print you've made on the record.

HTML version: https://www.3dprintlog.com/

3D Print Log is a free web app for keeping a record of your 3D printing: times, temperatures, filament, photos, and what actually came out of the printer. Prints are captured from your slicer as you print, in one place you can search years later.

## Who it's for

- **One printer on a desk.** You dialed in the temperature and speed six months ago. Log it once and it stays with the print: the photo, the duration, the filament you used, and the note about what went wrong the first time.
- **A bench full of printers.** Track each roll and bottle from first use to empty. See what is running low before you start a twelve-hour print, and stop guessing whether there is enough left on the spool to finish.
- **Anyone who wants the numbers.** Total print hours, material used, and how often prints actually succeed across every printer you own.
- **Anyone coming from a spreadsheet.** The slicer uploader sends the print the moment you slice it. File name, printer, material, layer height, and estimated time arrive already filled in.

## Features

- [Print history](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/prints.md): photos, print time, filament usage, status, ratings, notes, and the slicer settings, searchable by name, project, status, printer, filament, or date.
- [Material inventory](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/materials.md): every spool and resin bottle, with brand, color, cost, and the amount remaining.
- [Printers](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/printers.md): each machine, its prints, its maintenance history, and its success rate.
- [Projects](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/projects.md): group the prints of a multi-part build.
- [Analytics](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/analytics.md): totals, filament usage, print time, cost, and trends.
- [Achievements](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/achievements.md): badges for printing milestones and streaks.
- [Android app](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/android-app.md): log prints and check your filament from your phone.

## Integrations

- Slicers: [OrcaSlicer](https://www.3dprintlog.com/orcaslicer), [Bambu Studio](https://www.3dprintlog.com/bambu-studio), [PrusaSlicer](https://www.3dprintlog.com/prusaslicer), [Creality Print](https://www.3dprintlog.com/creality-print), and SuperSlicer use the [post-processing uploader](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/slic3r-uploader.md). [Cura](https://www.3dprintlog.com/cura) has a [Marketplace plugin](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/cura-plugin.md).
- Live print status: [OctoPrint](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/octoprint-webhook.md) and [Klipper/Moonraker](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/klipper.md).
- AI assistants: an [MCP server](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/mcp.md) for Claude, ChatGPT, and other MCP clients.
- Your own code: a [REST API](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/api.md) with personal API keys or OAuth 2.0. Agents should read [auth.md](https://www.3dprintlog.com/auth.md) first.

## Pricing

- **Free:** unlimited prints, printers, and materials, with up to 5 images per print. No credit card required.
- **[Pro](https://www.3dprintlog.com/docs/pro-subscription.md):** $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year. Removes ads, allows up to 20 images per print, and adds file attachments (G-code, STL, and 3MF).
