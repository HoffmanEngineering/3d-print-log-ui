title: 'Bridges & Resin Totals'
---

**Manage your printlog-bridge from the web.** A new [Bridges](/docs/printlog-bridge-bridges) screen (Printers menu, then Bridges) lists every bridge you've paired, whether it's online, and the printers it found on your network. Connect a printer in a couple of clicks, and enter its access code or password right on the page: it's encrypted in your browser to that bridge's own key, so 3D Print Log never sees it. If you run more than one bridge, a printer found by two of them is shown once, and you can move a printer from one bridge to another along with its credentials. These need printlog-bridge 0.2.0 or later, paired to your account.

**Pair and revoke devices.** Bridges now pair with your account through a short sign-in code instead of a pasted API key, and Settings, then Connected Agents, lists every paired device with a Revoke button (or Revoke all when you have several).

**More printers, and resin in mL.** The bridge has new guides for Bambu Lab, PrusaLink, Elegoo (SDCP), OctoPrint, Duet, FlashForge and Anycubic printers, each with its own connect setup. Resin prints now show their usage in mL on print and printer pages, and analytics and project totals show mL beside the grams.

#### Full List of Changes:

- **Bridges screen** - See each paired bridge, its status and version, and the printers it found on your network, then connect them from the list or the printer page. ([PR #382](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/382), [API PR #288](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/288))
- **Printer credentials sealed to your bridge** - Access codes, passwords and API keys are encrypted in your browser to the bridge's key, and you're asked to confirm when a bridge's key changes. ([PR #383](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/383), [API PR #289](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/289))
- **Several bridges** - A printer found by two bridges is shown once, and you can move a printer between bridges together with its credentials. ([PR #384](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/384), [API PR #290](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/290))
- **Paired devices** - Settings, then Connected Agents, lists your paired bridges with a Revoke button for each. ([PR #381](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/381), [API PR #286](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/286))
- **Revoke all devices** - With two or more paired devices, revoke them all at once. ([PR #391](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/391), [API PR #299](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/299))
- **Bambu Lab and PrusaLink** - New source labels, the printer software shown on each connection, a connect flow and guides for both. ([PR #379](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/379), [API PR #282](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/282))
- **Elegoo (SDCP) and OctoPrint guides** - Setup guides and connect setups for the [Elegoo](/docs/printlog-bridge-sdcp) and [OctoPrint](/docs/printlog-bridge-octoprint) adapters. ([PR #389](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/389))
- **Duet, FlashForge and Anycubic guides** - Setup guides and connect setups for the [Duet](/docs/printlog-bridge-duet), [FlashForge](/docs/printlog-bridge-flashforge) and [Anycubic](/docs/printlog-bridge-anycubic) adapters. ([PR #390](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/390), [API PR #298](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/298))
- **OctoPrint notice** - When the bridge watches a printer that OctoPrint's Webhooks plugin also reports, the notice now explains how to turn off the plugin's 3D Print Log hook. ([PR #387](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/387), [API PR #292](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/292))
- **Resin usage in mL** - Resin prints show their usage in mL on print and printer pages, never grams or meters. ([PR #386](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/386), [API PR #293](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/293))
- **Resin totals in analytics and projects** - mL appears beside the gram totals wherever you've logged resin. ([PR #388](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/388), [API PR #297](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/297))
- **mL everywhere** - Volume labels on settings, material and print forms now read "mL". ([PR #393](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/393))
- **Material Usage marked Other** - The [Printers](/docs/printers) guide explains how loading a roll, or picking one, fills a print's Other usage row. ([PR #422](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/422))
- **Better link previews** - Public prints, projects and profiles share their own title, description and image when linked, and list views load smaller thumbnails. ([PR #380](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/380), [API PR #285](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-api/pull/285))

([PR #408](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/408), [PR #410](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/410), [PR #414](https://github.com/HoffmanEngineering/3d-print-log-ui/pull/414))
